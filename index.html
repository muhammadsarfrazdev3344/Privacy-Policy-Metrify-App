Privacy Policy

Metrify - Smart Unit Converter

Effective date: 29 September 2026

In short

Metrify does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell your personal information. The app works completely offline, has no user accounts, and asks for no device permissions.

1About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Metrify Android application (package name com.sarfrazdigital.metrify), published by Sarfraz Digital ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the app.

2Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, Metrify does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account details
  • Your location or contacts
  • Photos, files, microphone, or camera data
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics
  • The values you type into the converter

3Data stored on your device

To make the app work, the following small pieces of data are saved locally on your device only. They are never uploaded or sent to us or to anyone else.

DataPurposeWhere stored
Favorite conversionsShow your saved conversions in the Favorites tabApp storage on your device
Theme choice (light / dark)Remember your preferred appearanceApp storage on your device

You can remove this data at any time by deleting favorites inside the app, clearing the app storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the app.

4Permissions

Metrify does not request any special Android permissions. It does not need internet access, and all conversions are calculated on your device.

5Third-party services

Metrify does not include third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services, and it does not share data with any third party.

6Copy to clipboard

When you tap the copy button, the converted result is placed on your device clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. This happens only when you choose to do it, and the app does not read your clipboard.

7Children's privacy

Metrify does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no personal data is collected, there is no child data to store or share.

8Data security

Because your data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security features such as screen lock and app sandboxing.

9Your rights

Since we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete. You stay in full control of the locally stored data described in section 3.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
